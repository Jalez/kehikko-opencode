- **A notice you have already dealt with stays dealt with.** Restarting the module no longer brings back a session's "finished" or "needs you" notice as if it were new. Only a new turn or a new question does that.
- **Open from the Inbox shows the session it is about.** When a place holds several sessions, opening one's Inbox item or notice puts that session on screen. Its "needs you" then clears, as it does when you look at it yourself.
