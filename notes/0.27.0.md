- **One clear conversation chooser.** The View menu now has a single "Conversation: …" chooser named after the conversation on screen. It shows this kehikko's own conversation and the other conversations here under their own headings, each with what it is doing (working, needs you, finished or idle) and when it was last active.
- **New and Disconnect in the same place.** "New conversation" and "Disconnect this conversation" are now at the end of the chooser. They are no longer spread across the View and Run menus.
- **No conversation list in Go.** The Go menu no longer lists conversations to connect, and with nothing left in it, it is gone. To connect a conversation you started elsewhere, type /kehikot-here in it.

## For agents

- The header's conversation items changed. The `conversation` switch now also takes `new-conversation` and `disconnect-conversation`. The `connect` jump, the `connected` toggle and the `disconnect` run are gone. None of these is pressable by agents (`press_item` refuses them, as before). Use `sessions` to read which conversations a place has.
