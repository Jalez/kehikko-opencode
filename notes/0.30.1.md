Kind: feature

- **Places read as spaces.** A session's place is now said as a space, such as "space Auth › Login", in the notices, the `/kehikot-here` answers, the conversation lists and the help, wherever it used to say an epic and its parts.
- **Deeper spaces keep their name.** When you stand in a space nested more than two levels down, the notice names the whole path rather than only its top two steps.

## For agents

- Your notes and message lines say where you are as a space (`space Auth › Login`); the older epic and parts words still work wherever you pass one.
- `sessions` takes a `space` (an id, or a path of ids such as `["auth", "login"]`) beside the older `epic`, and `place` takes a `space` path beside `epic` and `parts`. A session's `sameEpicAt` is also given as `sameSpaceAt`.
