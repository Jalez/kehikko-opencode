- **Codex on a kehikko.** A terminal holding the Codex CLI session that belongs to the epic, the parts you picked and the kehikko in front of you; a new one is filed there and is that kehikko's own, with nothing to type.
- **It knows where it is.** Each session is told its place, the modules on the kehikko and their tools, and the skills Kehikot loaded for it; a module added while you work is said at your next prompt.
- **Working, waiting for you, done.** What each session is doing shows in the Inbox and the View menu, and you are told when one you are not looking at needs you or finishes.
- **How full the context is.** A line under the session shows the window in use, with Compact and Clear one press away while Codex waits at its prompt.
- **Picked up after a restart.** A session is resumed when you come back to its place, and one a restart cut off mid-turn is said in the Inbox and told what happened when you open it.
