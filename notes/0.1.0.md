- **OpenCode on a kehikko.** A terminal holding the OpenCode session that belongs to the epic, the parts and the kehikko in front of you, resumed when you come back to the place.
- **It knows where it is.** Each session is told its place and the modules on its kehikko, and gets their doors and skills for that session only; nothing in your own OpenCode setup changes.
- **Working, needs you, finished.** The Inbox shows when a session is busy, waiting for an approval or an answer, or done, and plays "Research complete" when research finishes.
- **Context and cost at a glance.** The line under the session shows how full the context window is, the model and what the conversation has cost, with Compact and Clear on a press.
- **Install from the container.** If OpenCode is missing, one press installs it without admin rights.
