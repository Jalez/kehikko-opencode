Kind: feature

- **Adopting an item with OpenCode no longer fails with “did not answer within 8 s”.** OpenCode now takes the task at once and starts afterwards; the dialog closes straight away and the conversation appears in the OpenCode container as it starts. Before, OpenCode had to boot and name its conversation first, which could take up to a minute — far longer than Kehikot waits — and the conversation started anyway, unseen.
- **If OpenCode cannot start the task, the Inbox says why.**
- **A conversation placed with /kehikot-here can now say how its turn ends.** `tell_finished` takes the conversation's key, so its Inbox line shows done, a decision for you, or research complete.
