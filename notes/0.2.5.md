- **"Research complete" notices.** When a Codex session finishes researching something, or an explorer subagent (or one asked to investigate, compare or audit) finishes, the Inbox says "Research complete" with what was researched. In Kehikot's Settings it can have a chime of its own.
- **Sessions can say it themselves.** A session started from Kehikot is told it can mark a finished turn as research, so you hear about it when it ends. Opening the session puts the notice away, like any other finished item.
