- **A question reaches you as a question.** When a session stops to ask you something, the Inbox shows the question, with its choices, instead of "turn ended", and you can answer it right there.
- **Stalls are no longer "finished".** A session that stops without saying it finished or asking you anything shows as "Stopped without finishing", with its own sound — unless you were watching it stop.
- **Finished means finished.** A session that says its work is done, or that its research is complete, shows as done, with its own words.
