- **A small container starts its conversation too.** A OpenCode container too small for a terminal now starts the place's conversation behind its tile, so the tile shows a real session instead of nothing until you make the container larger. Like any conversation started here, it becomes the place's own once you say something in it, with nothing to type.
- **No more "type /kehikot-here" for a conversation started here.** While the new conversation this module started is on screen, the line above it says it becomes this kehikko's own once you speak to it. /kehikot-here is only for a conversation started elsewhere, such as in your own terminal.

## For agents

- `sessions` with a `place` that has none now says that opening the module there starts a new conversation at any container size, which is filed under that place as its own by itself once its person first says something in it; `/kehikot-here` is only for a conversation started outside the module.
