- **Disconnect from this kehikko sticks.** A conversation you disconnect stays disconnected: opening it again, its run going on, or OpenCode carrying it on under a new id no longer puts it back on the kehikko, and an older kehikko it was once on no longer claims it. Connect it again from the Go menu or with /kehikot-here.
- **/kehikot-here off.** Typed in a conversation, it disconnects that conversation from every kehikko, without its container on screen.
