- **A stalled session is reminded to say how it ended.** When a session stops without saying it finished, asking you anything or setting up a wake, it is reminded once, typed in at its prompt after a short pause, to tell you where it stands. Never when you typed or sent something since, never into a question waiting for you, and never twice without a prompt of yours between. Turn it off with "Remind the agent to say how its turn ended".

## For agents

- A turn about to stop without `tell_finished` or a wake set up is sent back once with a reminder: finish, ask with `needs-decision`, or set up what will wake you.
