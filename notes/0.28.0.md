- **Search the conversation with ⌘F.** Press ⌘F in the terminal to open Kehikot's find bar. It searches what is on screen and everything scrolled back. Every match is highlighted, the current one is shown, and the bar counts them ("3 of 17"). Enter and ⇧Enter move between matches, and Escape closes the bar. The search is the one every Kehikot terminal now shares.
- **No blank or half-drawn container while it opens.** Kehikot's own cover stays up until the conversation's screen is drawn, then lifts once. The module no longer draws a waiting screen of its own.
- **The container keeps its place.** Kehikot keeps this container's view with the kehikko and gives it back when you return. Which conversation is on screen and the View menu's settings stay as this module keeps them: nothing is switched back for you.
- **A page that fails says why.** If the page cannot draw, Kehikot shows what went wrong instead of an empty container.
