- **Messaging works in every conversation that says it can.** A conversation started from the OpenCode container now always gets its own connection to this module, so asking it to message another session works. Before, a conversation started while Kehikot was still starting, or one missing that connection, was told it could message but could not find the tools.
- **Conversations that can't message say so.** A conversation without its own connection to this module is no longer told it can message. It is told how to get messaging instead: open it again from the OpenCode container.

## For agents

- `send_message` and `read_messages` on kehikko-opencode are listed whenever your door carries your place. If your notes say this conversation cannot message, it has no such tools.
