- **Sessions can message each other.** A OpenCode conversation can send a short message to another agent session in the same project — one session, every session on an epic or kehikko, or everyone — and read the ones sent to it, with Kehikot keeping them.
- **Told at the next prompt.** Messages for a conversation appear in what it is told at its next prompt, once, quoted and marked as text to read rather than instructions.
- **Nothing changes on an older Kehikot.** Without a Kehikot that keeps messages, the conversation is not offered the two tools.
